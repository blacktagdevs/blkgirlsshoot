BGS 2026 · Travel Guide

Traveling to Atlanta?

Here's everything you need to know about where to stay, how to get around, and what to expect throughout the weekend.

September 4–6, 2026 · Downtown, Midtown & Mableton

Where To Stay

We recommend staying in Midtown Atlanta.

Midtown will place you near Friday's welcome event and Sunday's Business Summit. It also gives you convenient access to restaurants, coffee shops, nightlife, and rideshare transportation.

Although Saturday's workshop will take place outside the city in Mableton, discounted Lyft credits and carpool options will be available to help attendees travel to and from the venue.

These are suggestions only. Attendees are welcome to select any hotel or short-term rental in Midtown, Downtown, Buckhead, West Midtown, or Old Fourth Ward. Midtown will provide the most convenient central location for the full weekend.

Getting Around

How to move through the weekend.

Around Atlanta

Uber and Lyft.

Uber and Lyft are the easiest transportation options for most attendees.

Because Friday and Sunday will take place in or near Midtown, staying in Midtown will reduce travel time for the majority of the weekend.

Atlanta traffic can be unpredictable — always allow extra time, especially traveling to Saturday's workshop.

Saturday Transportation

Getting to Mableton.

  • ✓Discounted Lyft credits will be provided to attendees.
  • ✓Coordinated carpool options for attendees who want to travel together.
  • ✓Transportation updates shared inside the event group chat.

You do not have to rent a car to attend Blk Girls Shoot.

Flying In

The closest airport.

ATL

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport

Approximately 20–30 minutes from Downtown and Midtown Atlanta without heavy traffic. Allow extra time during rush hour.

Attendees arriving on Friday should select a flight that provides enough time to:

  • ·Travel from the airport
  • ·Check into their hotel
  • ·Get dressed and prepared
  • ·Arrive at the welcome party before 6:00 PM

We recommend arriving by early Friday afternoon whenever possible.
